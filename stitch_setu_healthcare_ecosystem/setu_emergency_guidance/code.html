First-Aid Protocols

pest_control

Snake Bite

  • Keep the patient calm and immobilize the bitten limb.
  • Do not cut, suck, or apply ice to the wound.
  • Remove tight clothing or jewelry near the bite area.
  • Arrange immediate transport to a facility with Anti-Snake Venom (ASV).
device_thermostat

High Fever (Pediatric/Adult)

  • Administer paracetamol as per age/weight guidelines.
  • Perform tepid sponging if temperature exceeds 103°F.
  • Ensure adequate fluid intake to prevent dehydration.
  • Monitor for danger signs (convulsions, lethargy).
water_drop

Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH)

  • Call for immediate assistance and arrange transport.
  • Perform continuous uterine massage.
  • Keep the mother warm and elevate legs if possible.
  • Prepare for IV fluids if trained and equipped.
